Frequently asked

QUICK ANSWERS

You can pause your membership for up to 3 months per year through the Member Portal under Account → Membership. To cancel, submit a request at least 7 days before your next billing date via the portal or by contacting our team. No cancellation fees apply on monthly plans — annual members may be subject to an early exit fee.

We're sorry about that! Duplicate charges can happen during payment method updates. Please contact our billing team via live chat or email with your transaction reference numbers. We process refunds within 3–5 business days and will confirm by email once initiated.

Premium and Elite members receive 2 complimentary guest passes per month. Additional passes can be purchased at the front desk for £10/day. Guests must be accompanied by the member at all times and sign a liability waiver on first visit. Guest access is not available during peak hours (6–9am, 5–8pm weekdays).

Personal training sessions can be booked via the Ashish Fitness Zone App under the "Train" tab. Select a trainer, browse their availability, and confirm. First-time PT sessions include a complimentary fitness assessment. If you have a package, your credits will be applied automatically at checkout.

A keycard that's not working usually means it needs to be re-encoded, which takes about 2 minutes at any reception desk. If your membership is active and the card still fails, you can use app-based QR code entry as a temporary workaround. Visit reception or contact us and we'll sort a replacement immediately.

Log into the Member Portal → Billing → Payment Methods → Add New. Your new card will be used for the next billing cycle. Old methods are not auto-deleted — you can manage multiple cards and set a default. For bank direct debit changes, please contact us directly as these require verification.
